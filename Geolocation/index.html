    Class: Geolocation

    A geographic location associated with a dataset: a single point, a bounding box, or a polygon. Coordinates are WGS 84 decimal degrees. This mirrors the OSTI E-Link 2.0 Geolocation structure, adding optional accuracy values. Many legacy coordinates were backfilled from place names or site descriptions, so leave the accuracy slots empty when the accuracy is not known rather than guessing.
